About this test

  • The model: drivendata/pasketti-phonetic on Hugging Face. It listens to speech and writes down the sounds it hears (IPA), not the words, so it can catch a wrong sound even when the result isn’t a real word.
  • The test plan: Pasketti spike checklist.
  • How it ran: on a Mac laptop, no cloud. Each recording is turned into sounds, then compared with what she said and what the book says. The model is right when its sounds are closer to what she actually said.
